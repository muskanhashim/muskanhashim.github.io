the night we met

August 03 2026

for a long time this question scared/scarred me:

should i have stayed in that night

took me long enough to realize/say it out loud. sure, everything probably happens for good reason and i wouldn't be where i am right now without everything that happened the way it did but if i could go back, i'd tell myself to stop trying to escape the dark, empty room and to conquer the demons within and behind me before looking to befriend more outside.

you'd think nothing would be uglier than the memories inside you: you'd be surprised. You don't need new bad memories to escape the old ones.

They don't get overwritten, it compounds.

make your prison prettier.

you have enough trauma. process that first.

update: monday september 7

I’ve long forgiven. I don’t think it matters - me staying in or not.

I’m glad I met you, and I’m grateful to have left you.

Thank you for loving me, in the only way you knew how to; so heavily, it couldn’t be contained without being broken. Thank you for taking care of me.

Unrelated but whenever I see a mark on my body, my mind scrambles and goes back asking who did it this time. I got a mark yesterday from my bike, similar to the one when you hit me. I like to think that all these marks I collect here and there would’ve been, in an alternate reality, the marks on my body from you, had I stayed. Isn’t it beautiful that I saved you the shame

And the hurt and guilt and regret and eventual avoidance and shamelessness that you’d otherwise have felt?
