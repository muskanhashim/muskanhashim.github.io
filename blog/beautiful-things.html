beautiful things

16 August 2025

a few months ago you said this song always came on the radio to haunt you after dropping me off to work. Today I realized how much pain you were carrying within you. During the sad moments, of course, when you felt alone, misunderstood, like the black sheep, but also even during the happy moments- like something inside you didn’t want to believe it was real or lasting. Or that the second you’d be happy or “yourself”, it would be taken away from you. I’m sorry the world made you feel that you couldn’t be yourself. I’m sorry I made you feel like that. I’m sorry the voices in your head were so damn loud and refused to shut up, no matter how hard you tried to drown them. I’m sorry the happy moments were edged with a black filter of fear, hurt, potential loss, and pain. I’m sorry your brain was wired to be so mean to you. Maybe that’s why you insisted on being so kind to others, because you knew how hard it was so fight negativity like that.

I’m sorry you were carrying so much pain within you and no one stopped to realize. I’m sorry the world kept misunderstanding your kindness for weakness and using you for it. I’m sorry it was never easy to be you but no one realized. I’m sorry no one realized how deep your wounds were and that you were bleeding inside the whole time. I’m sorry that the only thing that seemed to numb it down and quiet the voices and made you fit in was so mean to you, even as though you poured all your love into it- maybe a little more than you loved me. I’m sorry your screams for help drowned in the labels of alcohol addiction. It didn’t cause the pain, it seemed to alleviate it, until it started hurting more than it helped. I’m sorry it never seemed that life could just be “easy”.
