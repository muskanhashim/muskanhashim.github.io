the only thing i have is myself

I was in grade 10 when one of the guys who liked me punched a glass window at school because someone else asked me out.

Then there were the love letters etched out in blood in grade 12.

At 19, the 38 year old man who liked me wrote a book about me, and then left me in the country he flew me out to (because I asked).

Love is ugly, and it doesn’t exist.

My father keeps apologizing for abandoning me, thinking any amount of money would bring me my childhood back, or make me somehow act my age now.

Unfortunately, I’m wiser than that. It must be nice to lose yourself in love but I don’t have the heart to lose anymore than I have. The only thing I have is myself.
