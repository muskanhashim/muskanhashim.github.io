03

train station human

august 15 (heh) 2025

i see a alcoholic homeless human, who happens to be addicted to a substance that is slowly destroying his soul and body, at the train station every day. he started drinking early today; he had a one litre flask of beer in his hands. he really seemed like the happiest man on earth, sitting in a pool of his own urine on the ground outside the train station.

he started at 9 am because instead of buying him water, someone gave him enough money to buy his beer early on. yesterday he seemed sad and angry, almost agitated, and frustrated. even annoyed with me during our daily exchange. he couldn't give in to his addiction because he didn't have enough money to pour into the one thing that would make the world slightly more bearable for another 2 hours before allowing him to pass out into peace, until he would wake up again, hungrier for it.

it made me realize that sometimes we pray for things desperately, with the might of all our heart, believing that one thing will finally make us happy. because we don't know god's plan, we become agitated when we don't receive it, without realizing that not getting it may be his ultimate protection.

note: if you see a homeless human who happens to be addicted to alcohol, try talking to them or just offer the shortest smile. alcohol dehydrates you; buy them water. take that sandwich out of your bag, you probably need it less. one meal could be the buffer their stomach is begging for to stay alive.
